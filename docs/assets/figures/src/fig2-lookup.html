How to read it
A0 or AX: AIVSS equals CVSS-BTE. A1 or A2: read the cell for the CVSS-BTE band, then take the higher of that cell value and CVSS-BTE. AIVSS is therefore never lower than CVSS-BTE. Never interpolate between cells.
