Assessor checklist

SCOPE
1
Define one coherent exploit path (path_id). Score materially different paths separately.
2
Classify the finding as ASI01 to ASI10, or as MAESTRO-extended with layer and threat metadata.
CVSS v4.0
3
Resolve Exploit Maturity (E) from the evidence ladder KEV, Vulnrichment or observed exploitation, public PoC, otherwise E:U (Section 6.1)
4
Build the CVSS v4.0 vector and score it with the FIRST algorithm Label the score CVSS-B, CVSS-BT, CVSS-BE or CVSS-BTE by the groups assessed
AGENTIC METRICS
5
Assign LC, CP, AP, SR and ES; use X when evidence is missing Check that SR agrees with AT: SR:P or SR:U requires AT:P
6
Assign the Effect Class with the ordered ladder: A2, A1, AX, A0 Mark the result provisional when any of LC, CP, AP or SR is X
7
Read U from Table 5 and AIVSS from Table 6 AIVSS is never lower than CVSS-BTE
8
Record agent_untraceability from a retrieval exercise (High, Moderate, Low or X)
DECISION AND REPORT
9
Conformance Level 2: resolve the BOD 26-04 baseline; apply the A2 overlay if it applies Report the unmodified baseline beside the AIVSS recommendation
10
Publish CVSS-BTE, Effect Class, U, AIVSS and the provisional flag Never replace CVSS-BTE with AIVSS where CVSS is required
